Airport security screening trays

Heavy-duty passenger screening trays for airport checkpoint conveyors — stackable, impact-resistant and custom-branded to the operator’s livery (shown here in Abu Dhabi Airports Company / ADAC colours). Sized to fit standard X-ray tunnels and return-belt systems.

  • Impact-resistant polypropylene construction
  • Nestable / stackable to save belt-side space
  • Custom colour & printed branding (operator livery)
  • Sized for standard X-ray tunnel & return-belt systems
  • Smooth radius corners — easy cleaning, no snag points
  • Optional anti-slip base & RFID-tag pocket

“Follow Me” LED guidance boards

Roof-mounted programmable LED “FOLLOW ME” boards for airport marshalling and apron-guidance vehicles. High-brightness amber matrix readable in direct sunlight, paired with rotating beacons for airside visibility during aircraft and vehicle escort.

  • High-brightness amber LED matrix — sunlight readable
  • Programmable scrolling / static messages
  • Roof-bar mount with twin rotating beacons
  • 12/24 V airside vehicle power, weather-sealed (IP-rated)
  • Front & optional rear display faces
  • Wide operating-temperature range for apron use

// Frequently asked

Questions we hear most about Airport Equipment.

Can the screening trays be branded with our airport’s logo?
Yes — trays are produced in your chosen colour and printed with the operator’s livery. The reference photos show Abu Dhabi Airports Company (ADAC) branding; we print to any airport or ground-handler identity.
Will the trays fit our existing X-ray and return-belt system?
Trays are sized to standard X-ray tunnel and return-belt footprints. Send us your checkpoint make/model or tray dimensions and we confirm fit — bespoke sizing is available for non-standard lanes.
Are the “Follow Me” boards readable in UAE daylight?
Yes — the boards use a high-brightness amber LED matrix designed to stay legible in direct sunlight, with twin rotating beacons for airside visibility. They run on 12/24 V vehicle power and are weather-sealed for apron use.
What is the typical lead time?
Branded screening trays and LED boards typically ship within 2–6 weeks depending on quantity and the branding/print requirements. We confirm an exact window on quotation.
